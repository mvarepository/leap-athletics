About Leap

Jump testing shouldn’t be a luxury.

Leap Athletics supplies sports clubs and schools with vertical jump test equipment at an affordable price.

Most vane testers are priced for pro teams and university labs. We think every coach, PE teacher and athlete should be able to measure power — so we designed our own, and we’re making it available to everyone, not just organisations.
